## Pelvis-lowering factor

`reachable_pelvis_factor` (the `pelvis_height` decision): the generator has a flat foot, a pelvis of fixed orientation and no heel-toe roll, so the source's mean pelvis height can leave its stride unreachable. The nominal pelvis height is lowered from the source's mean until the rig's neutral is feasible, so the output walks with a lower pelvis than the source in order to reach the same stride (the G1 does the same: 0.68 m nominal below its 0.79 m straight-leg stand). ## Pitch-innermost shoulder exclusion

A driven hinge reproduces its BVH Euler channel, so when the shoulder pitch channel is its bone's innermost rotation channel it turns about the abducted arm's own lateral axis. Above a commanded abduction of 0.5 rad the extractor over-reads the arm swing and under-reads the abduction, so arm_swing_amp and arm_abduction are not gated on such a run (marked ungated in the tables).

Applies to: Bandai Namco dataset-2 walk_normal_001.

## Findings

- CMU units: the inference now knows the CMU ASF length unit (1/0.45 inch, 0.0564444 m) and picks it for this file from its 14.33-unit rest leg (0.809 m); Phase 4.6 read the file as inches, at 0.45 of its size (leg 0.387 m).
- Bandai units: position channels replace the OFFSET axis by axis, so the constant child columns that repeat their OFFSETs leave the bones at true size and the Hips' varying columns carry the travel; Phase 4.6's reader added them and read every bone at twice its length (leg 1.848 m).
- Bandai shoulders: the shoulder pitch channel is innermost and angry commands 0.483 rad of abduction, just under the 0.5 rad exclusion threshold, so the pitch-innermost coupling likely contributes to angry's arm_swing_amp and arm_abduction residuals (an infeasible run).
- CMU elbows: elbow_r is skewed (axis purity 0.72, below 0.8) and left unbound, so the elbow_flexion group is half-bound: the bound left elbow is driven, the right is held at the reference, and the extractor's two-arm means mix the two (elbow_flexion and elbow_swing_ratio beyond tolerance on every run: the documented half-bound limitation of posture_map).
- Short clips: every run is written for at least eight steps (four gait cycles) at its own cadence, so the 2.64 s CMU clip's sad run (cadence lowered) is written longer than the source and holds complete cycles; the per-run lengths are in each rig's facts.
- Angry and happy are infeasible on every real rig (the generator's feasibility flag is false; the pelvis factor is fitted to the neutral only); the arm and elbow fields beyond tolerance on the 100STYLE and Bandai rigs occur on those infeasible runs only.
- Slip on feasible runs: the neutral run of the 100STYLE full clip (2.9 mm travel) exceeds the 2 mm slip bound although feasible, because feasibility is a per-knot 2 mm position threshold while slip is a frame-to-frame measure over a spline-interpolated stance, so up to about twice the threshold can occur.
