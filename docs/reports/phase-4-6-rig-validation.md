## Pitch-innermost shoulder exclusion

A driven hinge reproduces its BVH Euler channel, so when the shoulder pitch channel is its bone's innermost rotation channel it turns about the abducted arm's own lateral axis. Above a commanded abduction of 0.5 rad the extractor over-reads the arm swing and under-reads the abduction, so arm_swing_amp and arm_abduction are not gated on such a run (marked ungated in the tables).

Applies to: Bandai Namco dataset-2 walk_normal_001, Bandai Namco walk_normal_001 at true size (check).

## Findings

- CMU units: the hip-to-ankle rule infers 0.0254 m per unit (inches), the known scale nearest the 0.8 m leg target, so the rig is read with a 0.387 m leg and a 0.401 m mean pelvis height. The file keeps the CMU ASF length unit (ASF `length 0.45`: one unit is 1/0.45 inch, 0.0564444 m, what the project's ASF reader uses; subject 104's ASF femur is 6.998 units, this file's thigh 6.92 units), which is not among the inference's known scales. The inference rule is unchanged; the check table re-runs the rig at --scale 0.0564444 (leg 0.861 m).
- Bandai units: inference chose 0.01 m per unit and up axis y (the file's own units and axis; the +x rest spine did not mislead it). Every joint has six channels whose constant position values repeat the OFFSETs; the BVH reader adds a position channel to the OFFSET, so every bone below the root is read at twice its length (mean pelvis height 1.709 m, leg 1.848 m). The constant columns are folded into the compiled offsets (rig.compile). The reader is unchanged; the check table re-runs the rig at --scale 0.005 (pelvis 0.855 m, leg 0.924 m).
- CMU sad: the clip is 2.64 s; sad commands 1.191 steps/s (neutral 1.832), and the output, the source's length, holds no cycle the extractor can use ({'no complete cycle': 1}), so every sad residual is NaN. An extractor limit on a short clip; nothing is retuned.
- CMU elbows: elbow_r is skewed (axis purity below 0.8) and left unbound, so the elbow_flexion group is half-bound: the bound left elbow is driven, the right is held at the reference, and the extractor's two-arm means mix the two (elbow_flexion and elbow_swing_ratio beyond tolerance on every run, at both scales: the documented half-bound limitation of posture_map).
- Angry and happy are infeasible on every real rig (the generator's feasibility flag is false; the pelvis factor is fitted to the neutral only); the arm and elbow fields beyond tolerance on the 100STYLE and Bandai rigs occur on those infeasible runs only.
- Infeasible presets are reported, not gated: on an infeasible run the generator's feet miss their targets, which is what the slip and height columns of those rows measure.
