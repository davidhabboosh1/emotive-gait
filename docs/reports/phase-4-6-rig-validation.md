## Pitch-innermost shoulder exclusion

A driven hinge reproduces its BVH Euler channel, so when the shoulder pitch channel is its bone's innermost rotation channel it turns about the abducted arm's own lateral axis. Above a commanded abduction of 0.5 rad the extractor over-reads the arm swing and under-reads the abduction, so arm_swing_amp and arm_abduction are not gated on such a run (marked ungated in the tables).

Applies to: Bandai Namco dataset-2 walk_normal_001, Bandai Namco walk_normal_001, bones at true size, root travel at half (check).

## Findings

- CMU units: the hip-to-ankle rule infers 0.0254 m per unit (inches), the known scale nearest the 0.8 m leg target, so the rig is read at 0.45 times its size (see its leg length and pelvis height above). The file keeps the CMU ASF length unit (ASF `length 0.45`: one unit is 1/0.45 inch, 0.0564444 m, what the project's ASF reader uses; subject 104's ASF femur is 6.998 units, this file's thigh 6.92 units), which is not among the inference's known scales. The inference rule is unchanged; the check table re-runs the rig at --scale 0.0564444.
- Bandai units: inference chose 0.01 m per unit and up axis y (the file's own units and axis; the +x rest spine did not mislead it). Every joint has six channels, and the constant position values of the bones below the pelvis repeat their OFFSETs; the BVH reader adds a position channel to the OFFSET, so every bone is read at twice its length (the pelvis height and leg length above). The constant columns are folded into the compiled offsets (rig.compile). The check table at --scale 0.005 has the bones at true size but also halves the root's varying Hips translation, so the source gait is read at half its step length and speed: bones at true size, root travel at half. A replace-offset reading of position channels would be the faithful re-read; that is a reader change, not made here.
- Bandai shoulders: the shoulder pitch channel is innermost and angry commands 0.483 rad of abduction, just under the 0.5 rad exclusion threshold, so the pitch-innermost coupling likely contributes to angry's arm_swing_amp and arm_abduction residuals (an infeasible run).
- CMU elbows: elbow_r is skewed (axis purity 0.72, below 0.8) and left unbound, so the elbow_flexion group is half-bound: the bound left elbow is driven, the right is held at the reference, and the extractor's two-arm means mix the two (elbow_flexion and elbow_swing_ratio beyond tolerance at both scales: the documented half-bound limitation of posture_map).
- Short clips: every run is written for at least eight steps (four gait cycles) at its own cadence, so the 2.64 s CMU clip's sad run (cadence lowered) is written longer than the source and holds complete cycles; the per-run lengths are in each rig's facts.
- Angry and happy are infeasible on every real rig (the generator's feasibility flag is false; the pelvis factor is fitted to the neutral only); the arm and elbow fields beyond tolerance on the 100STYLE and Bandai rigs occur on those infeasible runs only.
