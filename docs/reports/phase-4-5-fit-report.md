## Self-check

Synthetic self-check (6 actors, 4 cycles per clip, seeds 0-9): a planted anger cadence effect of 0.080 was recovered with mean estimate 0.080, and lay inside its 95 % Wald interval in 9 of 10 datasets (with six actors the interval is expected to be somewhat optimistic).

## Limitations

1. **Human effects, not robot perception.** The fit says how acted emotion changes human gait parameters. Whether a G1 showing those changes reads as the emotion is Phase 5's question.
2. **Intensity and mixing are assumptions**, not identified from these data: effects scale linearly with intensity and emotions add.
3. **Acted, single-intensity portrayals by mostly non-professional actors**, pooled across capture protocols. The dataset term absorbs level shifts, not protocol differences in kind.
4. **Speed is confounded with emotion** in every available set. The primary effect is "as acted"; the secondary fit shows what survives a speed covariate, and that is all that can be said.
5. **Fear is thin** (1 actor in this fit), **anger is thin** (1 actor in this fit), and **disgust and surprise are absent**. Thin cells carry an actor count instead of an interval and enter no headline number; the CLI warns.
6. **Transfer is approximate.** Froude similarity is a first-order rule (Donelan & Kram 1997); the G1 walks crouched with a bent knee and its joint limits clip some effects. The robot plays a rescaled, clipped version of a human effect; data value and played value are reported separately. Two rig constants sit between a learned posture delta and what the G1 shows: its elbow's geometric bend is 1.385 rad minus the joint angle, so a positive (more flexed) human `elbow_flexion` delta, such as happiness's, renders as straighter elbows; and the pelvis-to-neck vector pitches 0.88 of the commanded waist pitch, so a learned trunk effect renders at that gain and the measured head-on-trunk angle inherits the difference.
7. **The structure test is low-powered**: four emotions give five group means, so rank 3 is one dimension short of saturated. It is a model comparison, not a discovery.
8. **Label mapping is a declared assumption** for 100STYLE's Depressed and Elated and for CMU's style names; the artefact counts `mapped` actors per emotion.
9. **Operational definitions are the project's.** `elbow_swing_ratio`, `arm_swing_amp` and `neck_pitch` (head on trunk) are defined to match the generator, not to match any one paper's measure, so fitted values are not directly comparable with published numbers.
